२००६

Mrs. Laxmi Acharya starts making pickles in her kitchen in Chitwan, the way her grandmother did.

हाम्रो कथाThree generations. One kitchen. Same recipes.

Neighbours kept asking for more. Friends wanted jars for their families. Before long, what was a home recipe became something bigger.

Twenty years later, not much has changed. We still pick the ingredients ourselves and follow the same recipes. The kitchen is bigger, but the hands and the heart behind every jar are still the same family.

Every product is free of artificial colours, flavours and preservatives.
