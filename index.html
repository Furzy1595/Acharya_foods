२००६

Acharya Foods begins in a small kitchen in Chitwan, making pickles, spices and flours the traditional Nepali way.

हाम्रो कथाReal ingredients. Honest recipes. Made in Chitwan.

Neighbours kept asking for more. Friends wanted jars for their families. Before long, a small home kitchen grew into Acharya Foods.

Twenty years later, not much has changed. We still pick the ingredients ourselves and follow the same recipes. The kitchen is bigger, but the care that goes into every jar is the same.

Every product is free of artificial colours, flavours and preservatives.

  1. १आफैं छान्छौंWe pick the ingredients ourselves
  2. २हातले पिस्छौंSpices ground by hand
  3. ३धैर्यले पकाउँछौंIf it takes three days, we take three days
