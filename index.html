Our Story

Three generations. One kitchen. Same recipes.

It started in 2006 — Mrs. Laxmi Acharya in her kitchen in Chitwan, making pickles the way her grandmother did. Neighbours kept asking for more. Friends wanted jars for their families. Before long, what was a home recipe became something bigger.

Twenty years later, not much has changed. We still pick the ingredients ourselves. We still follow the same recipes. The kitchen is bigger, sure, but the hands and the heart behind every jar? Still the same family.

Every product we make is free of artificial colours, flavours, or preservatives. We don't believe in shortcuts — if it takes three days to get the pickle right, we take three days.
